# How to Play Belot (Official Rules)

**Belot** is a trick-taking card game for 4 players, played in two teams of two (partners sit opposite each other). The game uses a standard 32-card deck (a bridge deck with cards 2 through 6 removed).

The game proceeds in four main stages:
1. **Dealing**
2. **Bidding**
3. **Gameplay (Play)**
4. **Scoring**

---

## 1. Dealing

Cards are dealt in a **counter-clockwise** direction.
* Each player is initially dealt 3 cards, then 2 more cards (5 cards total per player).
* The remaining 3 cards per player (12 cards total) are kept on the side and dealt *after* the bidding phase concludes.

---

## 2. Bidding

The purpose of bidding is to determine the contract/game mode. The available game modes are:

### Game Modes & Card Rankings

1. **Trump Game (Suit Contract)**
   * One suit (Clubs, Diamonds, Hearts, or Spades) is selected as the dominant **trump** suit.
   * **Trump Suit Ranking (Descending):** `J` > `9` > `A` > `10` > `K` > `Q` > `8` > `7`
   * **Non-Trump Suit Ranking (Descending):** `A` > `10` > `K` > `Q` > `J` > `9` > `8` > `7`
   * Any card in the trump suit wins over any non-trump card, regardless of rank.

2. **No Trumps (No-Trump Contract)**
   * No suit is dominant.
   * **Card Ranking for All Suits (Descending):** `A` > `10` > `K` > `Q` > `J` > `9` > `8` > `7`

3. **All Trumps (All-Trump Contract)**
   * All suits act as trump suits.
   * **Card Ranking for All Suits (Descending):** `J` > `9` > `A` > `10` > `K` > `Q` > `8` > `7`
   * No single suit dominates another.

### Bidding Flow
* Bidding begins with the player to the **right of the dealer**.
* Players bid in counter-clockwise order, either proposing a game mode (a specific suit, "No Trumps", or "All Trumps") or calling **"Pass"**.
* A player can make a higher contract bid or pass. Contracts rank in ascending order:  
  `Suit (Clubs < Diamonds < Hearts < Spades)` < `No Trumps` < `All Trumps`.

#### Double (Contra) & Redouble (Recontra)
* If the latest valid bid was made by an opponent, a player may call **"Contra" (Double)**. This indicates intent to defeat the opponents' contract. All scores for the round are doubled.
* The opposing team may respond with **"Recontra" (Redouble)**, which quadruples all points for the round.
* Under Contra or Recontra, the team that wins the round takes all resulting points.

#### Concluding Bidding
* Bidding ends when **3 consecutive players pass**. The dealer then deals the remaining 3 cards to each player (totaling 8 cards per hand).
* If all 4 players pass during bidding without making a contract, the cards are recollected, and the player to the right becomes the new dealer.

---

## 3. Gameplay

Gameplay starts with the player to the **right of the dealer** playing a card face up (establishing the led suit). Each player follows counter-clockwise.

* The player who plays the highest-ranking card (or highest trump) wins the trick.
* The trick winner collects the cards face-down and leads the next trick.

### Movement Rules by Mode

* **Trump Game:**
  * Players **must follow suit**.
  * If a player lacks the led suit and the trick is currently held by an opponent, they **must play a trump** ("ruff" / "trump").
  * If an opponent has already played a trump, the player **must play a higher trump** ("overtrump") if possible. If they do not have a higher trump, they may play any card.
* **No Trumps:**
  * Players **must follow suit**. There are no trumps and no requirement to beat higher cards beyond following suit.
* **All Trumps:**
  * All suits are trumps. Players **must follow suit** AND **must play a higher card** than the current highest card in that suit, regardless of whether their partner or opponent is currently winning the trick.

---

## 4. Scoring & Card Values

At the end of the round, teams calculate the points gathered from their taken tricks based on the following values:

| Card | Trump Suit Points | Non-Trump Suit Points |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **7** | 0 | 0 |
| **8** | 0 | 0 |
| **9** | 14 | 0 |
| **10** | 10 | 10 |
| **J** | 20 | 2 |
| **Q** | 3 | 3 |
| **K** | 4 | 4 |
| **A** | 11 | 11 |

### Contract Outcomes
* **No Trumps Multiplier:** Points accumulated in "No Trumps" are doubled (except for Capo/Valat bonuses).
* **Made Contract ("Izkarana"):** If the bidding team scores more points than the opposing team, the contract succeeds. Both teams score their respective points.
* **Failed Contract ("Vkarana"):** If the bidding team scores fewer points than the opposing team, the bidding team receives 0 points, and the opposing team takes ALL points (including the declarers' points).
* **Hanging Game ("Visyashta"):** If scores are tied, the contract hangs. The bidding team receives 0 points for that round; their points are carried over and awarded to whichever team wins the subsequent round. The non-bidding team records their earned points normally.

### Round Point Totals & Rounding
* Base trick total (including "Last 10"):
  * **Trump Game / Suit:** 162 total points (recorded as 16).
  * **All Trumps:** 258 total points (recorded as 26).
  * **No Trumps:** 130 total points before doubling -> 260 total points (recorded as 26).
* Points are divided by 10 and rounded to the nearest integer.
* **All Trumps Rounding Rule:** If both teams end in scores ending in `4` (e.g., 154–154), the team with fewer raw trick points rounds up.
* **Hanging Points Rounding:** Hanging points round up for the next hand (e.g., in All Trumps, 15 points go to the non-declarer and 16 points remain hanging).

### Double / Quadruple & Match End
* **Contra / Recontra Scoring:** The winning team takes all doubled or quadrupled points for the round. If a doubled/quadrupled game ends in a tie (hanging), all doubled/quadrupled points roll over to the winner of the next round.
* **Winning Condition:** The first team to reach or exceed **151 points** wins the match. If both teams pass 151 simultaneously, the team with the higher score wins.
* **Capo Exception Rule ("S kapo ne se izliza"):** A team cannot win the overall match on a round that results in a Capo/Valat (winning all tricks) or a round where all players passed during bidding. Another round must be played.

## 5. Announcements & Bonuses (Anonsi)

Declarations are announced when a player plays their first card in the hand.

### Combination Announcements

| Combination | Name | Bonus Points |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| 3 consecutive cards of the same suit | **Tierce (Терца)** | 20 |
| 4 consecutive cards of the same suit | **Fifty (Кварта)** | 50 |
| 5 consecutive cards of the same suit | **Hundred (Квинта)** | 100 |
| Four 10s, Queens, Kings, or Aces | **Carre of 10/Q/K/A** | 100 |
| Four 9s | **Carre of 9s** | 150 |
| Four Jacks | **Carre of Jacks** | 200 |

* **Sequence Card Rank Order:** `7` < `8` < `9` < `10` < `J` < `Q` < `K` < `A`.
* **No Trumps Restriction:** No announcements (declarations) are permitted in No Trumps mode (except for Capo or Last 10).
* **Overlap:** If a card belongs to both a Carre and a sequence, the player must choose which combination to declare.
* **Higher Announcement Priority:** If both teams have sequence announcements, only the team with the higher sequence scores points:
  * Longer sequences beat shorter ones.
  * Equal length sequences are decided by the highest card.
  * If top sequences are completely equal on both sides, all sequence announcements for that hand are canceled.
* **Carre Priority:** Higher point-value Carres override lower ones. Carres do not cancel out sequence announcements.

### Special Bonuses
* **Belot (20 points):** Holding both the **Queen and King** of the trump suit (or any suit in "All Trumps"). Declared by saying "Belot" when playing the first card of the pair.
* **Last 10 ("Posledno 10") (10 points):** Awarded to the team that takes the 8th (final) trick.
* **Capo / Valat (90 bonus points):** Awarded if one team takes all 8 tricks in a single round.
